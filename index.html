The Artist

Hair, treated as fine art.

For more than two decades, Shondell Slaughter — known to her clients simply as Della — has turned hairdressing into a private, made-to-measure art.

From her chair at Main Street Parlor in Joshua, Texas, she creates lived-in color, seamless dimension and precision shapes designed around one thing: you. Every formula is mixed for your hair alone, and every appointment gets her full, unhurried attention.
